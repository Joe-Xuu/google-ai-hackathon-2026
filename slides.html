[ AI GOAL DECOMPOSITION ]

Gemini 3.0 Pro analyzes vague goals → structured JSON campaign blueprint

INPUT

"I want to lose 5kg in 2 months"

Difficulty: Normal / Hardcore

AI CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT

1️⃣ Extract duration: 60 days

2️⃣ Calculate daily quota: 500 kcal deficit

3️⃣ Set weekly targets: -1.25 kg/week

Weekly Milestones (4):

· Week 1 Checkpoint — verify 1.25kg loss

· Week 2 Midterm — verify 2.5kg total

Boss Raid (1):

· Day 60: Prove full 5kg loss → Legendary Title
